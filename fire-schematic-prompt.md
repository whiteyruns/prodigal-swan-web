## Prompt 1: Plumbing Flow Diagram

Create a clean technical plumbing schematic diagram for a propane fire effects system on an art car called "Prodigal Swan". White lines and labels on a dark blue blueprint background. Use standard plumbing symbols. Show the system as a left-to-right flow diagram with these components connected in order:

FUEL SOURCE PATH (left side): LPG propane tank (labeled '20 lb tank') → 1/2" to 1/4" reducer nipple → F-F regulator (low pressure LPG) → pressure gauge → 1/4" nipple → 1/4 turn ball valve → ~20 ft hose running to the vehicle body

ACCUMULATOR PATH (center): Hose enters vehicle → 1/4 turn ball valve (labeled 'driver accessible shutoff') → T-fitting → one branch goes to 0.3 gal accumulator tank, other branch continues to → solenoid valve (1/4 in, electrically triggered) → nipple → T-fitting → up through sealed steel neck

POOFER OUTPUT (right/top): Fuel line exits neck at crown → poofer nozzle (flame effect output)

PARTS LIST in a box in the corner:
- 12x 1/4" nipples (short)
- 4x T-fittings
- 1x 90° elbow
- 2x pressure gauges
- 3x 1/4 turn ball valves
- 1x solenoid (1/4 in)
- 1x low pressure LPG regulator
- 1x 0.3 gal accumulator
- ~20 ft hose
- Fittings: 1/4" M-NPT, M-POL

Style: clean engineering blueprint, labeled components, flow direction arrows, professional technical documentation quality.

## Prompt 2: Vehicle Side-Profile Overlay

> **TIP:** Attach page 1 of `swan_fire effects.pdf` as a reference image for the vehicle shape.

Create a SIDE PROFILE VIEW of the art car "Prodigal Swan" showing where each fire system component is physically located on the vehicle. Same dark blue blueprint style with white lines and labels.

The vehicle is a swan-shaped art car, viewed from the left side. It has:
- A tall curved SWAN NECK rising from the front-left of the vehicle body, reaching 11.5 feet tall
- A CROWN with pointed spikes at the very top of the neck
- A round EYE on the head
- The BODY is an enclosed ovoid/boat shape sitting on small wheels, about 16 feet long
- Large WINGS sweep back from the body on each side
- A human figure silhouette (6 ft) stands at the far left for scale, with a height scale showing 0 FT to 11.5 FT

Label these components IN THEIR PHYSICAL POSITIONS on the vehicle:
- "POOFER" at the crown/top of the neck (flame effect nozzle) with a small flame icon
- "FUEL LINE" shown as a red/highlighted line running DOWN through the inside of the sealed steel neck, from the poofer down to the body
- "FIRE EXTINGUISHER" mounted on the upper body near where the neck meets the body
- "ACCUMULATOR" (with box outline) at the front of the body, near the driver position, with label "1/4 turn accessible by driver to shut off accumulator"
- "FUEL" tank (with box outline) at the center-bottom of the body, labeled "protected by wing + steel guard", with a "1/4 turn to shut off fuel source" valve
- "BURN KIT" at the bottom/ground level near the fuel tank

Add a note at the bottom: "Fuel and fuel line is enclosed in a fully sealed, steel-plated neck piece."

Style: technical blueprint, clean engineering drawing, white lines on dark blue, labeled callouts with leader lines, height scale on the left side. The overall look should match a vehicle safety diagram for fire marshal review.
